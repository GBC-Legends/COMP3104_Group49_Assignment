SDLC - Software Development Life Cycle

there are 7 stages for SDLC.
planning, analysis, design, development, testing, deployment, maintenance.
they all work only when they are implemented together with an iterative approach.

Agile methodology is almost perfect for SDLC.
It has a lot of benefits. And it helps to improve the quality of software at every stage
by standardizing the administrative and management policies into a very concrete framework.
It helps projects to be more efficient and effective. It actually improves the percentage of projects
that are completed on time or even finished.
